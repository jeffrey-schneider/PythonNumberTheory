Note-->You asked how to professionally and ethically acknowledge ChatGPT’s assistance in a paper about the Integer Sequence Project, particularly where AI helped develop algorithms and functions.<--Note

The current academic norm is to treat AI assistance similarly to:

* a programming assistant,
* an editorial tool,
* or a research aide,

but **not** as a coauthor unless a journal explicitly allows it.

For a professional paper, the cleanest approach is usually:

1. Describe the human intellectual contribution as primary.
2. Acknowledge AI assistance transparently.
3. Specify what the AI assisted with.

A common wording is something like:

> Portions of the exploratory programming work and preliminary algorithm development were assisted using OpenAI's ChatGPT large language model. The author reviewed, tested, corrected, and integrated all resulting code and mathematical interpretations.

If you want something slightly more formal and computer-science oriented:

> OpenAI's ChatGPT was used as an interactive programming and ideation assistant during portions of the Integer Sequence Project, including exploratory pseudocode generation, debugging discussions, and refinement of Python implementations. All final algorithms, interpretations, testing, and validation were performed by the author.

For a mathematics-oriented paper:

> Computational experimentation and preliminary implementation discussions were assisted using OpenAI's ChatGPT language model. Final mathematical interpretation, verification, and sequence validation remain the responsibility of the author.

You can also include a citation entry.

APA-style example:

```text
OpenAI. (2026). ChatGPT (May 2026 version) [Large language model]. https://chat.openai.com/
```

Chicago-style:

```text
OpenAI. ChatGPT. May 2026 version. Large language model. https://chat.openai.com/.
```

In computer science papers, an acknowledgments section is often the best place:

```text
Acknowledgments

The author used OpenAI's ChatGPT language model during exploratory stages
of algorithm design and software implementation. The author independently
verified and validated all computational results.
```

The key professional principles are:

* disclose assistance,
* do not overstate AI originality,
* make clear you verified the work,
* maintain authorship responsibility yourself.

That last point matters because reviewers will care far more about:

* correctness,
* originality of the mathematical contribution,
* proofs,
* computational rigor,
* and reproducibility

than whether an AI helped draft a helper function.
